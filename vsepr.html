2 Steric number

Electron groups on the central atom: bonded atoms (m) + lone pairs (n). A double or triple bond counts as one group.

3 Lone pairs (n)

How many lone pairs stay on the central atom after every outer atom has a full octet (hydrogen: 2 electrons)?

Pick m and n

Rows: m bonded atoms. Columns: n lone pairs. Steric number = m + n.

Lone pairs sit where they have the most room: equatorial first for steric number 5, opposite each other for steric number 6.
