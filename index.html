It’s difficult to imagine many of the molecules we draw on paper, especially because molecular geometry is three-dimensional. Traditionally, we’ve used model kits to see them. I built these tools so students can easily draw a molecule and look at it from multiple angles, which helps them understand how molecules interact. I’m developing them with professors from UC Irvine to community colleges, and I’d love to keep doing this work at a community college, PUI, or state university.

Interactive tools

Org chem · Conformations Newman Navigator Type, draw, or pick a molecule and look straight down any bond. Turn the back carbon and watch the Newman projection and the energy change from anti to gauche to eclipsed. Students can draw wedges and dashes straight from their book. How I teach

  • Every session starts with me guiding you step by step through a problem, and ends with you guiding me through the same steps.

  • Mistakes are part of learning. I want you to make them here, so we can work out what separates a more correct answer from a less correct one.

  • I teach pattern recognition. The habits you build in chemistry carry over to any subject that asks you to reason from a small set of rules.

About me

I grew up in a household where my grandfather and my mother were educators, and a lot of my early learning came through storytelling. In my second year of college I took organic chemistry and was fascinated by how visual the subject is. Chemistry started clicking right away. At UC Riverside I worked as a Supplemental Instruction leader and tutor, where I learned to answer questions with questions, and that grew into my own inquiry-based way of teaching that has worked for many students. I carried that into my Ph.D. at UC Irvine, where I taught at every level of organic chemistry, and into an internship at Irvine Valley College, where I had the opportunity to teach a few lectures at the community college level and share my research with students. I have tutored students from Ivy League schools to state schools and can teach at whatever level of rigor your course demands. I’m looking for a permanent teaching position, and I tutor in the meantime.
